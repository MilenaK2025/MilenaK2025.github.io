Summer in New York

This endeavor is made a bit easier with the onset of summer, where in New York, the atmosphere once again relaxes. School is out; summer vacation begins; the weather becomes warmer; and spirits increase exponentially. Yet for me–and perhaps many others–summer brings a time of loneliness. However, what is so great about summer is that you have so much time to simply explore, to distract yourself from the fact that you are lonely. Enjoy sunset walks and solo beach days on Coney Island, bike rides along Rockaway, and even just getting some ice cream from your local Carvel. Watching runners panting along the boardwalk, girls walking their dogs in happy chatter, and hearing the laughter of children all ground you, reminding you that you are not alone. New York supports you, a New Yorker, in any way you find it can.
