Write Your New York

Our personal experiences influence our perspective of New York city. What makes New York so special? What aspects of New York make it the unique city that it is today?

When we live in New York for a long time, we notice things: things that might’ve slipped past the minds of others, or things that people might say you’re thinking about too much and wasting your time on. For me, it’s the seasons in this city: fall, winter, spring, and summer. Each one marks the beginning of some type of change, whether it be something you are yearning for or something that might inevitably punch you in the gut. Who decides? Well, fate flips a coin, and you are left with the consequences. The thing is, despite what happens, being in New York throughout these changes makes the concept of “change” somewhat more bearable. In this way, New York brings a unique experience into your life because of its fascinating culture—a culture that can only truly be observed and appreciated by noticing the small things.

As a New Yorker, I have experienced the wonder of the seasons within this city. New York, with its added touch and flair, accenuates these wonders and leaves you feeling content, nostalgic, emotional, and slightly distraught all at once. New York has the capability to pull at the heartstrings of even the most tenacious of hearts.
