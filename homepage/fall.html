Fall in New York

Once fall dawns upon New York, you notice a sudden plethora of consumerism. From that first drifting auburn leaf from the trees, you know the countdown to Starbucks releasing their Pumpkin Spice Lattes has begun. You go online and all you see are cinnamon-scented candles, pumpkin-themed bath bombs, fall-themed Trader Joe’s hauls, and cute little ghosts as coasters... Some people might think it is all a waste of money, these seemingly insignificant purchases, but a student who is already burnt out from the new school year finds themselves cracking a smile at the ghost coasters, or an exhausted mom finds some sort of peace in those cinnamon-scented candles. Bringing comfort is something that New York excels at. Because by romanticizing even the smallest of things, the burden brought by longer nights and colder winds is momentarily forgotten. Perhaps those "insignificant purchases" become the reason you find yourself smiling a little more, just a little.
